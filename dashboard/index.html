Bias checks and limits

Audit signals added; causal proof still needs a counterfactual

  • Portfolio mix: compare within stable DPD, risk, loan-type and balance strata.
  • Cohort maturity: do not compare vintages with unequal follow-up windows.
  • Selection / survivorship: retain all eligible accounts in the denominator.
  • Attribution window: a payment within seven days of a touch is association, not proof.
  • Time series: use completed months and compare against seasonality and operational changes.
05 / Counterfactual design

Counterfactual plan: estimate incremental targeting lift

Current channel associations are descriptive; the pilot below is how causal lift will be measured.

Experiment blueprint

Business-as-usual vs candidate targeting

  1. Freeze eligible accounts before assignment; exclude legal holds and policy-restricted cases.
  2. Randomize within DPD, risk, loan type, balance band and prior-contact strata.
  3. Log assignment and every delivered/attempted treatment; analyze by intent-to-treat.
  4. Primary outcome: net cash per eligible account within a fixed 30-day window.
  5. Guardrails: complaints, opt-outs, wrong-party contact, cost per incremental rupee and PTP breakage.

Decision rule

Scale only if evidence clears the hurdle

✓
Pre-register before launch

Define exclusions, sample size, stopping rules, payment-ledger logic and analysis before results are seen.

Treatment effect = average net recovery per eligible account in treatment − control. Scale only when the lower confidence bound clears the approved net-benefit hurdle and customer-protection guardrails pass.

SCENARIO MODEL

The base planning case estimates ₹12.79 Cr gross incremental recovery, ₹10.87 Cr realized economic benefit after an assumed 15% haircut, and ₹0.87 Cr net benefit after ₹10 Cr investment. This is an assumption-based scenario, not measured causal lift.

  • Downside / base / upside planning ROI: −45.7% / +8.7% / +63.0% (updated in model from the 85% realization assumption).
  • Break-even: ₹11.76 Cr gross incremental recovery, or about 9.19% lift on the assumed targetable cash base.
  • Illustrative budget allocation and model assumptions are documented in the investment report.
  • Release in stages; scale only after randomized results and guardrails pass.
