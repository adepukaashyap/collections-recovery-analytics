Targeting pilot: ready-to-populate design

This is a design and data-capture template, not a completed experiment.

Causal effect not measured

What has been prepared

  • Account-level planning frame with risk, DPD and balance strata.
  • Eligibility is deliberately marked REVIEW_REQUIRED; no assignment is made before business/Compliance rules are signed.
  • Outcome columns capture 30-day net cash, intervention cost and customer-protection guardrails.
  • Blank analysis template for treatment/control estimates and uncertainty.

Run protocol

  1. Approve eligibility and remove legal holds, disputes and restricted contacts.
  2. Freeze the eligible population and decide account vs borrower randomization.
  3. Randomize 50/50 within strata using a recorded seed and immutable assignment table.
  4. Log actual delivery/exposure and all costs; do not infer treatment from a recommendation.
  5. After 30 days, populate outcomes and run the pre-registered intent-to-treat analysis.

Do not report a causal lift yet

The provided dataset has no randomized arm, confirmed treatment exposure, 30-day experiment outcomes or intervention costs. Retrospective channel association is not a causal effect. A numeric sample size also requires an outcome variance and minimum economically meaningful effect.
