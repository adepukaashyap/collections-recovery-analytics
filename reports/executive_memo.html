Using current outstanding balance as a proxy for historical eligible balance would create false precision. Recovery rate should not be reported until Finance agrees a month-opening denominator, eligibility rules, write-off treatment and net-cash definition.

03 Why the claim is not proven

Measurement gaps

  • Less than 12 months of observations, with partial August.
  • No historical month-opening eligible balances.
  • Repeated and missing payment references; status changes require ledger reconciliation.
  • Recorded timezone semantics need source-owner validation.

Attribution gaps

  • Temporal proximity between a contact and a payment is not causal attribution.
  • No randomized holdout flag is supplied.
  • Portfolio mix, cohort maturity and selection can distort simple comparisons.
  • Intervention costs and incremental lift are absent, so ROI is not estimable.

04 Recommendation: stage the ₹10 Cr investment

Winner to test: better borrower targeting; stage the ₹10 Cr release.

Planning case (not causal proof): annualize seven complete months; assume 70% targetable cash, 10% base lift, 85% benefit realization and ₹10 Cr total cost. Base case: ₹12.79 Cr gross incremental recovery, ₹10.87 Cr realized benefit, ₹0.87 Cr net benefit and 8.7% planning ROI. Downside/base/upside ROI: −45.7% / +8.7% / +63.0%. Break-even is ₹11.76 Cr gross incremental recovery (9.19% lift on assumed targetable cash). Validate assumptions with Finance and randomized holdout before releasing full budget. 05 Decision metrics for the pilot

  • Primary outcome: incremental net cash recovered per eligible account over a pre-registered outcome window, relative to randomized holdout.
  • Financial outcome: incremental net recovery minus intervention and operating costs; calculate ROI and break-even only after causal lift and cost inputs are observed.
  • Guardrails: complaints, contact frequency, promise-to-pay quality, reversals, customer harm and risk-segment fairness.
  • Confidence: high that the supplied files do not substantiate a denominator-consistent 11% recovery-rate claim; low for causal strategy ranking and ROI before pilot data.
