METRIC GOVERNANCE

Metric Dictionary & Definitions

Definitions are versioned contracts. Every metric must state its numerator, denominator, window and limitations.

Cash metric

Recovered amount

Sum of amounts on canonical payment transactions whose final recorded state is SUCCESS, after documented deduplication.

Use: monitor cash movement. Do not call it: recovery rate.

Rate metric

Recovery rate

Net cash recovered in the outcome window divided by the approved eligible opening balance for the same population and period.

Current status: not reliably computable; historical opening balance is absent.

Trend metric

Month-on-month growth

(current completed-month amount / prior completed-month amount − 1) × 100

Compare completed months only. August 2026 is partial and must not be compared as a full month.

Attribution

Channel / targeting association

Payment occurring within a defined window after a recorded touch is temporal association only. It is not incremental lift or causal channel conversion.
