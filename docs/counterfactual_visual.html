EXPERIMENT DESIGN

Counterfactual & Investment Decision

A practical identification strategy to estimate what recovery would have looked like without the targeting change.

Current conclusion: the supplied files do not identify causal lift from the targeting change. The correct next step is a pre-registered randomized pilot, not an invented ROI estimate.

Recommended experiment flow

1. Freeze eligible population
→
2. Stratify by risk / DPD / loan type
→
3. Randomize treatment vs business-as-usual
→
4. Measure net recovery at day 30

Treatment group

Eligible accounts assigned to the candidate targeting strategy before outreach. Record assignment and all delivered/attempted touches.

Control group

Comparable eligible accounts assigned to current business-as-usual strategy during the same calendar window.

Primary outcome

Net cash recovered per eligible account over 30 days, including reversals/refunds under Finance-approved ledger rules.

Guardrails

Complaints, wrong-party contact, opt-outs, promise breakage, cost per incremental rupee and capacity impact.

Analysis and decision gates
