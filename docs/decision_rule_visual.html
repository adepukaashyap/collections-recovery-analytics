Pre-register the experiment and decision thresholds before results are observed. The current observational dataset cannot estimate causal lift.

01

Decision path

GATE 1
✎
Pre-register

Freeze eligibility, exclusions, sample size, primary outcome, stopping rules and ledger logic.

GATE 2
⚖
Run fair test

Randomize eligible accounts (or borrowers if spillover exists) to business-as-usual vs candidate strategy.

GATE 3
↗
Estimate lift

Compare 30-day net cash recovered per eligible account; report the intent-to-treat estimate and confidence interval.

GATE 4
✓
Apply guardrails

Check complaints, wrong-party contact, opt-outs, cost per incremental rupee and operational capacity.

02

Decision outcomes

Scale

Evidence clears hurdle

Only if the lower confidence bound for incremental net benefit exceeds the approved hurdle and all customer-protection guardrails pass.

Iterate

Promising but uncertain

If estimates are inconclusive or confidence intervals cross the hurdle, refine the strategy or gather more evidence without claiming success.

Stop

Harm or negative value

Stop or pause if customer-protection guardrails fail or evidence indicates negative incremental net benefit.

03

Core formulas

Treatment effect
Treatment − Control

Difference in average net recovery per eligible account over the pre-specified window.

Incremental contribution
Lift × margin − cost

Use Finance-approved contribution margin and incremental operating cost.

ROI
Contribution ÷ investment

Do not calculate a point estimate until causal lift and cost inputs are available.
